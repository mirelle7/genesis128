Genesis can be extended and customized in several ways. The following pages describe the possibilities of end users. Documentation on how to extend the program by writing code will be added later on.

* **Templates: **You can define your own [templates](Templates) for villages or resources that will be inserted into the generated maps.

* **Sceneries: **You can define your own [sceneries](Sceneries) for your generated maps. Sceneries determine the general look of your maps.
